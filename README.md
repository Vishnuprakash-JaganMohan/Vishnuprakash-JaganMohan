## 01. About Me

I'm an **Embedded Systems Master's student at Technische Universität Chemnitz, Germany**, with 4.4 years of industrial R&D experience in embedded hardware, firmware development, PCB design, and industrial automation.

My engineering background combines hands-on electronics development with microcontroller programming and hardware-software integration. I have designed multilayer PCBs, developed Embedded C firmware, integrated industrial communication protocols, and supported product validation and debugging.

Currently, I'm expanding my expertise toward **automotive electronics, embedded software, advanced PCB design, and hardware-software co-design**.

My approach is simple: understand the system, design reliable hardware, develop efficient firmware, and validate the complete solution.

### Engineering Interests

- **Embedded Hardware:** Schematic design, PCB layout, component selection, BOM, Gerber generation, and prototyping.
- **Embedded Software:** Embedded C, microcontroller peripherals, firmware debugging, and real-time control.
- **Automotive Electronics:** ECU concepts, automotive communication, sensor systems, and electronic control units.
- **PCB Engineering:** Multilayer design, design for manufacturability, and hardware validation.
- **Control Systems:** Motor control, sensor interfaces, industrial automation, and embedded control architectures.
- **Research & Development:** Prototyping, testing, root-cause analysis, and system-level integration.

### Currently Open To

- Working Student positions in Embedded Hardware or Software
- Embedded Electronics and PCB Design internships
- Automotive Electronics and ECU Development opportunities
- Research Assistant positions in embedded systems
- Engineering projects involving microcontrollers, sensors, and control systems
